Neural Music Fingerprinting implementation: https://github.com/raraz15/neural-music-fp (source LICENSE GPLv3 included).
Pretrained weights: Zenodo record15719945, nmfp-triplet.zip; separate distribution metadata is retained. Do not infer the weight license from the source-code license.
PQ8 fast scanner: https://github.com/mstsirkin/pq8-fastscan (MIT license included).
TensorFlow Lite: https://github.com/tensorflow/tensorflow (Apache2.0 license).
This app includes independently ported preprocessing/recognition and a catalog of metadata and fingerprints, not the source music collection.
App source and build/export/validation tools accompany the APK delivery.
